For grown-ups

Overview and how to use this page

This activity teaches 60 vocabulary words for a fourth grader, in 10 themed sets of 6. The words, meanings, sentences, and spelling notes were written for this activity. They are not from a particular school list or curriculum, so please skim them and adjust if your school has its own list.

  • Learn shows the word, its meaning, and a sentence, then walks through: say it, look closely, spell it, hide it, try it, check it, use it.
  • What does it mean? shows a word and four meanings. A wrong choice is shown next to the right meaning, and the word comes back once more in the round.
  • Spell it hides the word. The child sees the meaning and a sentence with a blank, and can hear the word. A wrong try stays on screen next to the correct spelling with the letters to notice highlighted. Then the child tries again from memory.
  • Paper spelling with a grown-up: you see the word and meaning, say the word, and mark the result after the child writes it.
  • Meaning and spelling progress are tracked separately. One correct answer does not make a word “known.”
  • No timers, scores against others, or penalties.

Hearing words: the “Hear the word” button uses the device’s built-in voice, if there is one. Voices vary. If it is not available, the page asks a grown-up to say the word. Nothing plays automatically.

Keyboard clues: autocorrect, spell-check, and automatic capital letters are turned off for the answer box where the browser allows it, but a tablet keyboard may still show word suggestions. If that is a problem, use paper mode.

Privacy: nothing is sent anywhere. Progress stays on this device, in this browser, unless you save the progress file and share it yourself. Browser storage can be cleared, so use “Save progress” for a backup. No names or personal details are collected.
